Privacy Policy for Maze Realm
Last Updated: January 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use the "Maze Realm" mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect
A. Information You Provide
We collect information you provide directly to us when you use the App:

Authentication Data: If you sign in with Google or Apple, we collect your authentication ID and basic profile information (such as name or email) solely for the purpose of securing your account and syncing your progress across devices. You may also use the App as a "Guest" without providing any personal information.
Game Progress: We store your game levels completed, scores, unlocked items (Pets), and achievements to provide the game service.
B. Automatically Collected Information
When you use the App, we may collect certain information automatically:

Device Information: We collect information about the device you use to access the App, including the hardware model, operating system and version, and unique device identifiers (e.g., IDFV).
Usage Information: We collect information about your use of the App, such as button taps, levels played, and error logs. This helps us improve the game stability and experience.
2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:

Provide, maintain, and improve the App.
Sync your game progress across multiple devices (if you sign in).
Participate in global leaderboards (using your chosen display name).
Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities in connection with the App.
Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions and other illegal activities.
3. Third-Party Services
We use trusted third-party services to help us operate the App. These services may collect information sent by your device:

A. Google Firebase
We use Firebase (provided by Google) for backend services:

Firebase Authentication: To manage user accounts securely.
Cloud Firestore: To store game data and leaderboards.
Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics: To analyze game performance and crash reports. This data is aggregated and anonymized.
Google Privacy Policy
4. Children’s Privacy
Maze Realm is designed for the whole family, including children.

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 outside of the persistent identifiers (like device ID) needed to support the internal operations of the App (such as saving game progress).
We do not show behavioral or targeted advertising.
We do not sell user data to third parties.
If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided us with personal information without their consent, please contact us at 
support@mazerealm.app
, and we will delete such information.
5. Data Retention and Deletion
We retain your game data as long as your account is active.
Deleting Your Data: You can request deletion of your account and all associated data directly within the App settings by tapping "Delete Account" or by contacting us at 
support@mazerealm.app
.
6. Security
We take reasonable measures to help protect information about you from loss, theft, misuse and unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration and destruction. All data transmission is encrypted using SSL/TLS.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will notify you by revising the date at the top of the policy and, in some cases, we may provide you with additional notice (such as adding a statement to our homepage or sending you a notification).
