Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
1. How do I restore my purchase?
Maze Realm is currently free to play! If we add in-app purchases in the future, a "Restore Purchases" button will be available in the Settings menu.

2. My game is crashing or freezing.
Please try the following:

Force close the app and restart it.
Check for updates in the App Store.
Restart your device.
If the issue persists, please email us with your device model and iOS version.
3. How do I save my progress?
Guest Mode: Progress is saved locally on your device. Warning: If you delete the app, you may lose your progress.
Signed In: If you sign in with Apple or Google, your progress is safely backed up to the cloud and can be synced across devices.
4. Can I play offline?
Yes! Maze Realm works completely offline. You can generate and solve mazes without an internet connection. An internet connection is only required for saving progress to the cloud or viewing global leaderboards.

5. How do I delete my account?
You can delete your account and all associated data at any time:

Go to the Settings screen in the app.
Tap on "Delete Account".
Confirm your choice. This action is permanent.
